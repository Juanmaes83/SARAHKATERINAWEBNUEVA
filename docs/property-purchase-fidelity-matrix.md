## Template elements deliberately not reproduced as facts

- `160+` foreign buyers, response in one working day and every service duration.
- The three package prices and any implication that tax/legal work is included
  without review.
- The three testimonial quotes, locations, years, outcomes and the `EUR 24,000`
  avoided-cost result.
- A real BUY / RENEGOTIATE / WALK AWAY recommendation. The implemented surface
  is labelled illustrative and contains no client result.
- Property Management or VITA Host in the ownership chain.
- A reconstructed service logo, Sarah signature, contact channel or legal
  entity.

## Review method

Human review must compare the template and preview side by side at 375 px and
1440 px, then inspect 320, 390, 768 and 1024 px for reflow. A matching headline
alone is not acceptance: section silhouette, media weight, rules, card
proportions, negative space and editorial rhythm are all part of the review.

## Post-merge fidelity check

The merge resolution changes no Property Purchase section order, copy, media
ratio or component silhouette. Its seven-stage file tracker, six-step process,
illustrative pre-sign dashboard and landing-specific FAQ/footer content remain
in place. The pending differences in the matrix above are unchanged: approved
photography, governed proof, destinations and human visual approval are still
required.
